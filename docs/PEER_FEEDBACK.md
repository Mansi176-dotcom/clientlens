# Practical peer review (not yet completed)

Ask a peer to use the dashboard for 15–20 minutes without coaching. Give them these tasks: explain the selected cohort/cutoff; find an account with severe support; show the evidence; distinguish exposure from saved revenue; explain why a demand gap is uncertain. Separately ask them to label the blinded request sample.

Record what actually happened:

Do not describe the solo build as cross-functional collaboration. After a real peer exercise, describe who reviewed what, what disagreement arose, and what changed. A feedback log is evidence of a review, not proof of stakeholder adoption.
